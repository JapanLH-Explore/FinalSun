Stay on their tail. Watch the convoy.

Final Sun is a story-driven arcade space shooter with cockpit-view combat and touch flight controls. Line up your shots, launch missiles and boost through danger while protecting the ships counting on you.

You have a wingmate.

Order Rook to cover you, attack your target, defend the convoy or intercept bombers. When threats arrive from every direction, decide where you need him most.

A desperate escape. A much larger secret.

Fly through ice rings, strange clouds and embattled star systems. Follow radio conversations, investigate unfamiliar signals and discover why the enemy refuses to let these survivors go.

Start with nine free missions.

Play missions 1–9 free, in sequence. A single, permanent in-app purchase unlocks missions 10–18 and the epilogue. The campaign continues in sequence as you complete each mission.

Designed for iPhone in landscape, with saved campaign progress, adjustable graphics and English and Japanese language options in Settings.
