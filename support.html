Player support

Need a hand, pilot?

Find help below, or send a question or bug report to the developer.

Email support

Email finalsun.support@gmail.com. You can write in English or Japanese.

Which missions are free?

Missions 1–9 are free, including mission 9. Complete each mission to unlock the next. A single, permanent campaign purchase unlocks access to missions 10–18 and the epilogue; you still progress through them in sequence.

I bought the campaign, but it looks locked.

Open Unlock Full Campaign from the main menu and choose Restore Purchases on the purchase screen. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase and check your internet connection. If access is still missing, contact support with the message shown on screen. You do not need to purchase the campaign again.

Where is my progress saved?

Campaign progress saves on your device. Keep the app installed when updating it. Deleting the app can remove local progress. Restoring a purchase restores campaign ownership; it does not restore mission progress.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings from the main menu and choose English or 日本語. The app uses your preferred device language on first launch when supported, then remembers your choice.

Where are the controls and graphics options?

Open Settings → Flight Manual for the controls. Graphics and sound options are also available in Settings. If the game is running slowly, try the lower graphics setting and close other demanding apps.

Report a bug or ask a question

Use the support link above. For a bug, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The Final Sun app version.
  • The mission number and what happened.
  • What you were doing just before the problem.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.

Avoid deleting or reinstalling the game while troubleshooting a save problem.

日本語のサポート案内

ご質問や不具合は、メールのサポート窓口へお送りください。日本語でも受け付けています。メールアドレス：finalsun.support@gmail.com

購入の復元：メインメニューのキャンペーン解放ボタンから購入画面を開き、「購入を復元」を選んでください。購入時と同じApple Accountを使用し、インターネットに接続してください。

セーブデータ：進行状況は端末内に保存されます。アプリを削除すると失われる場合があります。購入の復元では、ミッションの進行状況は復元されません。

言語・操作説明：メインメニューの「設定」から言語を変更できます。操作方法は設定内のフライトマニュアルをご覧ください。

不具合のご報告には、iPhoneの機種、iOSとアプリのバージョン、ミッション番号、発生した状況を添えていただけると助かります。
